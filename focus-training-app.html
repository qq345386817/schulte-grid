Focus training app

Schulte Grid for iPhone, iPad, and Mac

Schulte Grid is a lightweight focus training app built around a clear exercise: scan the grid, find each number in order, and measure the round with a timer.

Short sessions that fit real use

The app is designed for quick attention warm-ups before work, short study breaks, reading resets, and visual search drills that take only a few minutes.

Features

  • Multiple Schulte grid sizes from quick warm-up rounds to harder visual search sessions.
  • Timer and restart for repeatable practice.
  • Local history for recent completed rounds.
  • Printable practice sheets for offline use.
  • Support for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
