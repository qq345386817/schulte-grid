Privacy Policy

Simple, local, private.

Schulte Grid is designed to work without collecting personal data. Your training stays on your device.

Effective date: Jun 23, 2024. Last updated: May 20, 2026.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, keystrokes, training history, or device activity. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Schulte Grid works as a local practice tool. If you choose to contact support by email, your email address and message are used only to respond to your request.

Local storage

The app may store settings, recent practice data, and preferences locally on your device so the training experience works smoothly. This data is not uploaded to our servers.

Analytics and third parties

We do not use analytics services, attribution SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking pixels. We do not sell or share usage data with third parties.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for general attention practice. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the app, contact cameoshell09@gmail.com.
