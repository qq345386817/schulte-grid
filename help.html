Help

Use Schulte Grid

Schulte Grid keeps the exercise simple: choose a grid size, find the numbers in order, review your time, and repeat when you want a short focus reset.

Start a timed round

Open the Schulte Grid tab, choose the grid size you want, then tap the numbers in ascending order. The timer helps you compare sessions without adding complicated setup.

Choose a grid size

Use 3x3 or 4x4 for a short warm-up. Use 5x5 for classic Schulte grid practice. Move to 6x6 when you want a harder visual search session.

Print practice sheets

Use the print option to create clean offline sheets. Printed Schulte tables are useful for classrooms, screen-free practice, and quick desk exercises.

Review history

After you finish rounds, your recent results can help you see whether your practice speed is changing over time. History stays local on your device.

Install Schulte Grid

Download Schulte Grid on iPhone, iPad, and Mac from the App Store.
